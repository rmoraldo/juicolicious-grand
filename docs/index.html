Installer blocked? It isn't code signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn before running it. Here's how to open it.

Windows

  1. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, open the download menu and choose Keep.
  2. On the Windows protected your PC screen, click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS

  1. Open the installer. When macOS says it can't be opened, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

About

Juicolicious Grand is a sampled grand piano built from real recordings, playable in any DAW that supports VST3 or AU plugins, or on its own as a standalone app.

  • Four velocity layers per note, blended so tone and volume follow how hard you play.
  • Damper release sounds. Lifting a key plays the soft thump of the damper landing on the string, while the highest keys have no dampers and keep ringing, just like on a real piano.
  • Sustain pedal and Attack and Release knobs for shaping how notes start and end.
  • Play three ways: with a MIDI keyboard, your computer keyboard, or a mouse.
